A Communication-Avoiding Parallel Spectral Clustering Pipeline

In progress

MPhil Thesis · NIMS-KNUST · Supervisor: Dr. Elliot S. Menkah · Jan 2026–present

Developing a spectral clustering pipeline for graph Laplacians using a communication-avoiding Krylov method — a CA-Lanczos kernel that reduces synchronisation and global-reduction costs. The classical Lanczos kernel has been implemented in C and validated for eigenvalue accuracy against SciPy's eigsh symmetric eigensolver, ahead of extending to the s-step communication-avoiding formulation.
