I'm currently pursuing an MPhil in Scientific Computing and Industrial Modelling at NIMS, KNUST, building on a BSc in Mathematics. My thesis develops a communication-avoiding parallel spectral clustering pipeline — implementing a CA-Lanczos kernel in C to reduce synchronisation costs when computing eigenvectors of large graph Laplacians, validated against SciPy's eigensolver.
